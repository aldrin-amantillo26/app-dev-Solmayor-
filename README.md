## Plot
Rurouni Kenshin is a Japanese action-adventure film about Kenshin Himura, a former deadly assassin who vows never to kill again after Japan's civil war. Living as a wandering swordsman, he uses his skills to protect innocent people and help those in need while struggling with the violent sins of his past. When a dangerous criminal threatens the peace, Kenshin must confront his history and fight to defend the new life he has chosen.
